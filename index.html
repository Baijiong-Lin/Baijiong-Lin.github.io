Biography

Baijiong Lin (林百炅) is a Researcher at Lightspeed Studios, Tencent, working on controllable and interactive video generation models for games. His earlier research focused on multi-task learning and multi-objective optimization, with recent work on language agents.

He received his Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) in October 2026, supervised by Prof. Ying-Cong Chen.
